The problem

Creators send hundreds of messages hoping a brand will reply. Brands scroll through endless profiles with no idea who is real, what they charge or whether they'll turn up. Both sides lose time, and good people get missed.

What we built

A calm, fair place with a public leaderboard, honest reviews and direct chat. Creators rise by their work, brands by how they treat creators, and nobody can pay to rank higher.

Quick answers

Is it really free?

Yes. Joining, ranking, showing reels and getting offers cost nothing right now. We'll always tell you well before that changes.

Who can see my profile?

Only people who are signed in to {{ SITE_NAME }}. Your email is never shown to anyone.

Who can see my phone number?

Nobody, unless you choose. It is shared with a brand only after you accept their offer.

How is my rank decided?

By a public score from your audience, engagement, a complete page, reviews and reels. Money never changes your rank.

Do I pay through {{ SITE_NAME }}?

No. You and the brand agree payment between yourselves. We never ask for money, cards or UPI PINs.

What if someone behaves badly?

Press Report on their profile, review or chat. We review it, usually within a day, and can remove content or suspend accounts.
