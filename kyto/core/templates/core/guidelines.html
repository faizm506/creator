Community Guidelines

{{ SITE_NAME }} works because people trust each other. Here is what keeps it that way.

Do

  • Use your real details, real numbers and real reels.
  • Say clearly when a post is paid. Use Instagram's Paid partnership label or #ad. Indian advertising and consumer rules require it.
  • Reply to offers politely, even if the answer is no. Ghosting is not a personality.
  • Agree price and work in writing inside the chat.
  • Leave honest reviews after a finished job.

Don't

  • Don't fake followers, engagement or reviews, or pay for reviews.
  • Don't impersonate anyone or copy someone else's work or photos.
  • Don't harass, threaten, shame or discriminate against anyone.
  • Don't post anything illegal, hateful, sexual or violent, or anything involving a person under 18.
  • Don't send spam, scams or asks for money to "unlock" a deal. Never share OTPs, UPI PINs or card details.
  • Don't scrape the site or try to break it.

See something wrong?

Use the Report button on any profile, review or chat. A real person looks at every report, usually within a day. Serious complaints are handled by our Grievance Officer.

What happens if rules are broken

We may remove content, lower or reset a ranking, warn, or suspend an account. For anything illegal we may inform the authorities. You can ask us to reconsider through the Grievance Officer.
