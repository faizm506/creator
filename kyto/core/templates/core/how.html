How ranking works.

One simple score, the same rules for everyone, nothing hidden.

Creator score, out of 100

  • Audience size (up to 30). More followers earn more points, with diminishing returns so small creators can compete.
  • Engagement (up to 20). Likes and comments compared with your followers.
  • Complete profile (up to 20). Name, bio, niche, city, followers, engagement and price.
  • Reel showcase (up to 10). Add three reels to earn it all.
  • Brand reviews (up to 15). New profiles start in the middle. Good reviews lift you, poor ones lower you.
  • Verified (5). We check your Instagram and add the Kite Mark.

Brand score, out of 100

  • Creator reviews (up to 40). Brands that treat creators well rise. Poor reviews push a brand down.
  • Complete shop page (up to 25). About, city, address and shop photos.
  • Verified shop (20). We check the shop is real and add the Kite Mark.
  • Finished deals (up to 15). Three points for each job completed.

Reviews are real

You can only review someone after you've worked together and the job is marked as finished. Each person can leave one review for another person, and a newer one replaces the old one. A single review can't make or break a score, because ratings are blended with a neutral starting point until a few reviews come in.

Who goes first?

The highest score is number one. If two creators have the same score, the one with more followers goes first. If that is also equal, whoever joined earlier goes first. The order is the same for everyone.

It's free for now

Profiles, rankings, reels and offers are free while we grow. Nobody can pay for a higher score, and we'll tell you well before anything changes.
